Our Programs

Four rooms.
One roof. One pind at a time.

Explore the programs that bring the 360° model to life in every village we serve. Every program is designed with the input of village leaders and local partners to ensure cultural relevance and long-term viability.

local_library Room 01

A library in every village.

Modern knowledge hubs providing access to literature, education, and community dialogue — opening a window to the world for children who had none before.

IT lab & skills, bridging the divide.

Comprehensive training in software, internet, and vocational trades so young people can build a life in their pind — or anywhere they choose — without leaving family behind.

Health clinic & service desk.

Regular check-ups, health education, and a service desk helping villagers navigate government schemes and digital governance — closing the gap between need and access.

Vision Statement

A rural Punjab where the village your family came from is not a place of departure but a place of return — with the dignity, services, and opportunity of a modern town.

flag

Mission Statement

Inspired by the Shaheedi of Guru Arjan Dev Ji and the Seva of Bhagat Puran Singh, Mera Pind 360 bridges villages with opportunity — preserving culture and historic values while delivering IT and traditional skills, remote education, tele-health, and urban-grade civic services. Building self-reliant communities rooted in heritage, united in humanity, and ready for the future.

From the announcement at Chandigarh Press Club in 2021 to sixty villages today — a record still being written, one return at a time.

November 2021

The model is announced.

At the Chandigarh Press Club, Bhupinder Singh Hundal announces a new model for rural Punjab — four rooms, one roof.

29 March 2022

Foundation registered.

Incorporated at Village Kotli Arian, Adampur, Jalandhar. Library, IT lab, clinic, and service desk all operational by year-end.

2023–24

The model scales.

Sixty villages reach an agreement with the Foundation. 4,463 families screened and 14,000+ young people engaged in 2024 alone.
