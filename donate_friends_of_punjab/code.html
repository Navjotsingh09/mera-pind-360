About Friends of Punjab

Friends of Punjab Foundation is the US-registered 501(c)(3) arm that enables American donors to support the Mera Pind 360 initiative in rural Punjab, India — with full tax deductibility.

100% of programme funds reach the ground. Administrative costs are covered by the founding board, so every dollar you give builds villages.
